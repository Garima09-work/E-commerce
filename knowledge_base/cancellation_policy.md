# Order Cancellation Policy

Customers can cancel an order free of charge at any stage before the shipment has been dispatched from our fulfillment center. Once an order status transitions to Shipped or In-Transit, direct digital cancellation is disabled, and the customer should refuse delivery at the doorstep. For cancelled prepaid orders paid via credit card, debit card, UPI, or net banking, refunds are initiated immediately and reflect in the original payment source within 3 to 5 business days.
