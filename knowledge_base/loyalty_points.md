# Nykaa Rewards and Loyalty Points Policy

Customers earn 1 Nykaa Reward Point for every 100 rupees spent on completed and delivered orders across all product categories. Accumulated reward points can be redeemed during checkout, where each point offers a direct discount of 1 rupee on eligible catalog purchases. Loyalty points remain valid for 12 months from the date of credit and automatically lapse if unutilized. If an order is returned or refunded, the reward points credited for that transaction are debited from the customer's loyalty balance.
