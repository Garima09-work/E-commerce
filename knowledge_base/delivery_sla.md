# Delivery SLA Policy

Standard delivery across metro cities is fulfilled within 2 to 4 business days from the order placement date. Non-metro locations, tier-2, and tier-3 regions typically require 4 to 7 business days depending on courier accessibility and regional logistics. Express next-day delivery is available for select pin codes in Tier-1 metropolitan areas on orders placed before 2:00 PM IST. Any shipment experiencing dispatch delays exceeding 48 hours is automatically flagged in the logistics tracking system with an updated delivery estimate.
